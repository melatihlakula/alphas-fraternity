About ALPHAS

Our Foundation

Founded in 2025 by Melati Achuma Hlakula at Rhodes University, South Africa, ALPHAS represents the pinnacle of male leadership and brotherhood.

Our Mission

To cultivate exceptional young men who embody the values of leadership, integrity, strength, and brotherhood. We forge bonds that last a lifetime while developing the skills necessary to lead in an ever-changing world.

Core Pillars

The Foundation of Excellence

ALPHAS is built upon three fundamental pillars that define our character and guide our actions. These principles are not just words - they are the essence of who we are and what we strive to become.

DISCIPLINE

The unwavering commitment to self-control, consistency, and excellence in all aspects of life. We train our minds and bodies to achieve peak performance through rigorous practice and dedication.

LEADERSHIP

The ability to inspire, guide, and empower others to reach their full potential. We lead by example, demonstrating integrity, courage, and vision in everything we do.

SERVICE

The commitment to giving back to our community and supporting those in need. We use our skills, resources, and influence to create positive change in the world around us.
